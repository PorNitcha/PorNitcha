## 3. The strategy I would run: profit mix first, volume second

**Commercial — reprice the base, then grow it**
1. Floor by reaction on full cost, signed before any growth tonne ships. Lifting every customer×reaction line to GP/t 4,000 = ~THB 66M annualised; to 3,000 = ~42M. The deck's own fix-price list is worth 63M. This is the plan.
2. Cap XL at ~13–14kt unless it clears the floor. No Huifa at 17.92: it is below full cost (18.96) and at best zero contribution, and it sets a price umbrella on 3,000 t of growth. Indonesia's 3 accounts (−21.9M YoY) get a USD-indexed reset, not a visit.
3. Growth budget goes to Noodle, Bakery, Dairy, SSDM solutions (GP/t 4.3–6.8k) and to the 12 "grow with margin" accounts. Meat growth only at the floor. Cut the 43-account new-customer list to the 10 accounts ≥300 t with a dated first PO; the other 33 (120 t average, 15 under 100 t) stay in CRM, not in the plan.
4. Contract mechanics, not negotiation: native-indexed monthly pricing (native + mark-up by reaction) written into the top-12 contracts with quarterly reset. The 2026 loss happened because cost moved monthly and price moved annually.

**Production / supply chain — give each plant a role**
5. TKT = long-run, high-DS HP and PO-XL (Larox advantage), not an XL dump. Challenge the XL-only Jan–Jun sequence: PO-XL is at 98% of capacity while XL sits at 83%. Every month TKT runs PO-XL earlier is worth ~1.2 THB/kg of mix.
6. Rayong = short-run, specialty, high-reaction (AC, OX, AD, DD, Others, trials, new SNs) plus waxy/OEM refill. Decide its 2028 cost base now: at 32kt the fixed base has to drop to ~15M/month (MS1 and MS2), or Rayong drifts to 8 THB/kg and looks uncompetitive by default.
7. One demand number, allocation by GP/t after variable cost, not by plant convenience. Bring stock down from ~9.4kt to ~5kt (≈ THB 100M+ cash) before TKT adds its own pipeline.

**R&D / TSTD — fewer platforms, measured by first PO**
8. 14 projects / 27 SKUs in three quarters while two plants start is too many. Keep three: high-DS HP / HP-XL for bakery-noodle-dairy (TKT), AC-XL for surimi-frozen-pet (Rayong), clean-label HMT base for 2028. Park sago, pea, waxy corn unless OEM-sourced.
9. KPI = first-PO conversions and GP/t of converted volume, not SKU count or trial count.

## 5. Decisions to put in front of the CEO on 14 Oct

1. GP/kg, not tonnes, as the 2027 commitment (≥ 5.0 full cost; 2025 was 5.32).
2. Price floors by reaction signed before Q1 quotes; Huifa no-go below the floor.
3. TKT commissioning sequence re-cut to bring PO-XL forward.
4. Rayong 2028 cost base decision by Q2 2027, with a named owner.
5. New-customer plan reduced to dated, ≥300 t accounts; the rest tracked in CRM.

# Part 3 — Tracking GP/kg by tier within each reaction (added 15-Oct-2026)

Basis: product GP per kg = net sales − Actual Cost (column AF) − F&I in the profitability file, i.e. before idle cost, PPA and other adjustments (Por, 19 Oct 2026); the same basis as the AP27 reaction targets and the AP "data" sheet GP/kg. Tables in Parts 2–3 were built before the Other Adj add-back (about 0.1–0.2 THB/kg); the tracker v4 and the quadrant page carry the restated figures. 2025 full year is the anchor; 2026 Jan–Sep is the cross-check. Working file: `HVA GP tier tracker 2027.xlsx` (Tier definition · 2025 actual · 2026 YTD · 2027 target mix · Line list · Monthly tracking).

## 1. Design principles

1. **Unit = customer × SN (reaction) × producing plant.** The customer pays one price whichever plant ships, so the plant difference in GP/kg is cost, not price. Each line carries two numbers: commercial GP/kg at Rayong standard cost (sales performance) and actual plant GP/kg (allocation performance). Sales is judged on the first, Operations and allocation on the second.
2. **Five tiers per reaction, anchored on floor and target.** T1 unacceptable · T2 below floor · T3 floor-to-target · T4 target band · T5 premium. Floor = lower bound of T3; target band = T4.
3. **The tier bands sit on 2027 standard cost.** A customer's 2025 tier tells us the price position they accepted when price still covered cost. Returning them to that tier in 2027 means restoring the 2025 price relationship at 2027 cost, which is what the AP already assumes (XL ASP 26.6 THB/kg in AP27 versus 21.3 in 2025).

## 5. The monthly tracking routine (Finance BP)

1. **Day 3**: paste the month's invoice lines (customer × SN × plant) from the profitability file into "Monthly tracking"; load the month's standard product cost per reaction per plant.
2. **Compute** commercial GP/kg (Rayong cost) and plant GP/kg; assign tiers from the bands.
3. **Report** per reaction × plant: tonnes by tier vs the proposed mix, tonne-weighted GP/kg vs the AP target, and a migration list (lines that moved down a tier, lines in T1/T2). Each listed line gets an owner, an action and a date.
4. **Pass-through index**: month's own-starch cost (CAG) vs month's ASP by reaction. A rising CAG with flat ASP is the early warning that next quarter's tiers will slip, as in Q2 2026.
5. **Gate new lines**: a new customer × SN enters the plan only when its first invoice lands in T3 or above; until then it is pipeline, not volume.
6. **Link to approvals**: quotes landing in T1 default to no-go; T2 needs SD approval with a sunset date; T3 is approved with a dated reprice; T4/T5 are automatic. This maps onto the Pricing tool's 15% / 8% GP corridors.

Caveats: 2025 margins are at 2025 cost and serve as price-position proxies; the TKT cost advantages are taken from the AP gap between plants and need Finance confirmation; the plant split per line follows the AP's SN-ratio allocation, so the TKT figures move once the allocation is re-cut by customer.
