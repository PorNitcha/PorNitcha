Customer quadrant on a 2025 base

The AP deck classifies customers on 2026 GP per tonne at full cost and 2027 growth versus 2026. This page uses product GP (net sales less Actual Cost and freight & insurance, so before idle cost, PPA and other adjustments; the same basis as the AP reaction targets) and lets you rebase to 2025, to see which accounts sat on the floor before the 2026 cost shock, which fell through it in 2026, and how much of the 2027 growth is only a return to 2025 volume.

Lessons from 2025 → 2026 that set the 2027 room

The fix-price book sat on the floor in 2025, then fell through it
4,000–4,900 → 1,400–4,000

On product GP the big bidding accounts earned 4,026–4,943 THB/t in 2025 (CP Indonesia 4,026, Chunghom 4,241, SunChina 4,327, Indo Asia Tirta 4,358, Barentz 4,431, Tate & Lyle 4,943), all under the 4,700 floor; Huifa was negative. In 2026 they earn 1,447–3,967. The 2025-base fix book is 7 accounts, 22.3 kt, 35% of the plan: GP 60M in 2025, 26M in nine months of 2026.

So: "restore the 2025 margin" only returns them to the line. The 2027 target for this book is the floor on the plant that makes them, not 2025.
Cost moved, price did not, everywhere
+1,533 vs +212

Across accounts with history, product cost per tonne rose 1,533 THB and price 212 (tonne-weighted), so product GP/t fell 1,321. The grow accounts lost as much as the bidders: Golden Harvest 8,197→6,321, Shineroad 8,523→5,632, Tokai Denpun 11,516→7,523, ITOCHU 9,121→7,390, Morii 11,958→9,763.

So: the 2026 loss was a pass-through failure across the whole book, not a few bad accounts. The fix is a cost-indexed clause, not account visits.
2027 "growth" is partly win-back
31%

Of the 4.4 kt the grow accounts add versus annualised 2026, 1.4 kt only returns them to their 2025 volume. Accounts the deck calls "protect" on a 2026 base were shrinking in 2026 and are planned back toward 2025 (Samyang, Golden Harvest, Lucky KT, Universal Canning, ITOCHU).

So: price those tonnes as recovery of lost share, which needs service and spec work, not as new business that needs a discount.
Related-party margin moved the most
6,094 → 3,052

TW Indonesia is the only large account that crossed from grow to fix-price between the two bases: price −1,321, product cost +1,720 per tonne on 4,270 t planned. The 2026-based quadrant counts it as a customer to reprice.

So: set the transfer price at the arm's-length floor and report third-party GP separately, so the quadrant shows real customers only.
Over half the plan has no proven margin at the floor
56%

Fix-price book 22.3 kt (35%) plus 56 accounts with no 2025 history 13.4 kt (21%). The seven small accounts lost since 2025 (964 t) averaged 8,402 THB/t product GP.

So: the control point is a signed price on the fix book and a first-PO date on the new book before the volume is counted. Small high-margin accounts deserve protection, not neglect.
