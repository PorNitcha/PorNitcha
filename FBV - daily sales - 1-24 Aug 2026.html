What it shows
Realisation swings between 22 and 45 THB/kg day to day against a month-to-date 23.4. That spread is mix, not pricing — a day weighted to MT (33.3 THB/kg) prices out very differently from a day weighted to WSF (21.6). It is the clearest daily signal in the file that channel mix, not rate, drives what this category earns.

Source and basis

One file, one basis. Every figure on this page comes from Daily Sales Dashboard (2026 Food BU)_ 1-24 Aug 2026.xlsb — the 21,277-line billing extract on the 1-24 Aug sheet, cross-checked against the workbook’s own Data sheet. Nothing is drawn from the master P&L file or the Profitability Report.

Definitions. Sales = Net Val(After Disc) THB. Volume = Billing qty in SKU where the base unit of measure is KG, so it is kilos of product as billed — not the dry-basis tonnage used in the P&L files, and not comparable to it. Realisation = sales ÷ those kilos. Invoice discount = 1 − (Net Val After Disc ÷ Net Val Before Disc), i.e. the ZD-series discount on the billing document only; it is not trade agreement spend, which this file does not carry. Channel is derived from Sales Group, with Wholesale Fresh and Van Sales separated by salesman code (TF* = WSF). The category split reproduces the dashboard’s own category columns exactly.

Landing estimate = MTD ÷ 20 billing days elapsed × 26 billing days in August. Sundays excluded — the four in this period bill a fraction of a normal day; 12 August is kept in because it still billed. Linear, no seasonality.

Deliberately not shown. Gross profit, gross margin, trade spend, monthly history before August 2026 and any category-level target or last-year comparison. The daily sales file does not contain them at category level — it carries target, LE and last year by channel and salesman only, and no margin at all. Anything of that kind would have to come from the master P&L file or the Profitability Report, which are out of scope for this page by request.
