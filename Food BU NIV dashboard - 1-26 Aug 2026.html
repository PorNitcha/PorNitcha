Source and basis

NIV only, in THB million. Every money figure is Net Val(After Disc) THB shown in millions to one or two decimals. No gross, no margin, no volume.

One source: Daily Sales Dashboard (2026 Food BU)_ 1-26 Aug 2026.xlsb — the 23,277-line billing extract, plus the workbook’s own Data sheet for August target / LE / last year and its TG sheet for the closed months of 2026 and 2025. Nothing from the master P&L file or the Profitability Report.

MTG = month to go: the 4 billing days left in August (27–29 and 31; the 30th is a Sunday). MTG balance = target − MTD — what still has to be billed to land on target, which is what section 1’s hollow bars and sections 2.1 and 3 show. MTG estimate = MTD ÷ 22 elapsed billing days applied to each remaining day — what is likely at the current rate. The two are different questions and the page never mixes them in one bar.

The three dashed lines in section 1 are all NIV per billing day, read off the box top right. MTD RR = MTD ÷ 22 elapsed August billing days. MTG balance RR = (target − MTD) ÷ 4 remaining billing days. YTD RR = (Jan–Jul actual + August MTD) ÷ (182 + 22) billing days.

Caveat on YTD RR: the daily file holds line detail for August only — January to July exist as monthly totals. So the 182 billing days for those months are a calendar derivation (all days less Sundays), not a count of days that actually invoiced. Around Songkran and New Year the real count is lower, which means the true YTD run rate is a little higher than the 204-day figure shown. If you give me the company’s non-billing calendar for Jan–Jul I will use the real day count. The YTD line is also only drawn when the selection maps onto the monthly history: all categories, MT all-or-nothing, WSF and VAN together (the history keeps them merged as GTF) — otherwise there is no honest YTD for that slice and no line is drawn.

The 7-Eleven and Makro tabs exist because target and last year do reach MT customer level in the workbook. So those tabs carry a real MTG balance and a real growth number, but no YTD or AP — the monthly history and the AP both stop at channel and salesman. Their “% of BU cat.” column is the customer’s share of the whole Food BU category, and the categories they buy nothing of are listed under the chart.

Target by selection in section 1: target exists by channel, salesman and MT customer, never by category, so the hollow bars can only be an MTG balance when every category is ticked; filter to a category and they revert to the run-rate estimate, with the subtitle saying so. When all seven MT groups are ticked the MT channel target is used, which is 0.79 mn more than the seven named groups added up, so a full selection reconciles to the Food BU target of 224.5 mn exactly.

Growth is measured on landing, not on MTD, because the file holds last year by month only — there is no last-year daily figure to compare 24 days against. So “% growth” is landing estimate versus last year’s full month (MTD row) and versus last year’s Jan–Aug (YTD row). Comparing MTD against a full month would read artificially low.

YTD = the seven closed months of 2026 (Jan–Jul, from the TG sheet) plus August month to date. YTD is measured against AP, not target — AP Jan–Aug is the sum of the eight monthly AP figures in the TG sheet. The MTD boxes and sections 2 and 3 still run on target, because the file phases target by month at channel, salesman and MT customer level while AP is only phased at Food BU and channel level.

Which AP: the TG sheet holds two locked AP versions, labelled 2,360 MB (cols 97–108) and 2,377 MB (cols 111–122), both marked “don’t allow to adjusted”. This dashboard uses version 2,377 MB: Food BU full-year AP 2,777 mn, Jan–Aug 1,838 mn. Version 2,360 MB is 4.8 mn lower over Jan–Aug, which moves YTD achievement by 0.2 percentage points — not material, but stated so the number can be traced.

Where target does not exist: the file carries target, LE and last year by channel, salesman and MT customer — never by category. On the Ready and FBV tabs, and in section 2.2 everywhere, the hollow bar is therefore the run-rate estimate, not a target gap, and the YTD boxes are blank because the file has no category history before August 2026.

Modern Trade grouping follows the master file exactly: 7-Eleven, Makro, Lotus, Big C, CJ, TFF Mart, Other MT. The seven customer targets sum to 56.9 mn, which is the workbook’s MT – Customer line; the MT channel target is 57.7 mn, a 0.79 mn difference that sits outside the named customers in the source file.
