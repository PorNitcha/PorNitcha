Source and basis

NIV only. Every monetary figure on both tabs is NIV — Net Val(After Disc) THB from the billing extract. Nothing on this page is on a gross, pre-discount or margin basis. Volume, realisation per kilo and invoice discount have been removed at your request, so there is a single money measure throughout and no basis to reconcile between sections.

One source. Daily Sales Dashboard (2026 Food BU)_ 1-24 Aug 2026.xlsb — the 21,277-line billing extract on the 1-24 Aug sheet, cross-checked to the workbook’s own Data sheet. Nothing is drawn from the master P&L file or the Profitability Report.

Modern Trade grouping follows the master file (Thaiwah_Channel_PnL_by_Category_por.xlsx) exactly: 7-Eleven, Makro, Lotus, Big C, CJ, TFF Mart, Other MT. Top’s, Villa, PT, Maxvalu, Foodland, One Bangkok, Em & Earn, Jiffy, Lawson, The Mall, MM Mega, GO Wholesale and Golden Place all roll into Other MT. The seven groups sum to the Modern Trade channel total of 46,360,120 exactly.

Channel is derived from Sales Group, with Wholesale Fresh and Van Sales separated by salesman code (TF* = WSF). Ready and FBV together are 57.9% of Food BU NIV this month.

Landing estimate = MTD ÷ 20 billing days elapsed × 26 billing days in August. Sundays are excluded — the four in this period bill a small fraction of a normal day; 12 August is kept in because it still billed. Linear, no seasonality, and least reliable for Export and ASN where one shipment moves the month.

Not shown, by request: gross profit, gross margin, trade spend, volume, realisation per kilo, invoice discount, monthly history before August 2026, and category-level target or last-year comparison. The daily sales file does not carry margin at all, and carries target, LE and last year by channel and salesman only — never by category.
