Read this
FBV is the profit engine of Food TH: 36% of revenue, 46% of gross profit. It has earned 39–45% gross margin every month for 19 months, with realisation held between THB 67.5k and 70.7k per dry tonne and trade spend never above 8.2% of NIV (typically 1–5%). There is no pricing problem and no trade-spend problem here.
The one lever
FBV margin is a channel-mix question, and nothing else. Wholesale Fresh earns 38% and is 63% of the category; Modern Trade earns 58% and is only 20%. Same product, 20 points apart. In August the mix moved the wrong way — WSF up to 67%, MT down to 18% — costing about 0.5 points of gross margin, roughly 0.4 mn of gross profit at the August run rate.

What it shows
The amber line is flat and the two others move together. Realisation has held inside a 4.8% band for 19 months, so NIV is simply volume × a constant. December 2025 is the one real outlier — 1,456 t and 100.1 mn — and it was volume, not price. That makes FBV the easiest category in the portfolio to forecast, and it means the August -7% is a tonnage question with no pricing component to investigate.

Where the money is
7-Eleven earns 66% on FBV — the best margin of any outlet in the category, and the mirror image of what it does to Ready. Lotus’s at 60% and TFF Mart at 57% are close behind. This is the small-pack effect: the 200 g pack sold into Modern Trade realises 42.6–43.4 THB per kilo against 24.0 for the same pack in general trade.
FBV NIV by channel, THB mn · the mix has barely moved in 19 months — WSF 57–68%, MT 18–25%
The prize
Moving five points of FBV mix from WSF to Modern Trade is worth roughly 0.8 mn of gross profit a month, or about 10 mn a year, on the same tonnage. That is the single largest identified margin lever in Food TH and it needs no new product, no new price and no new capacity — only shelf.

5 · What to do

  1. Treat FBV mix as a formal target, not an outcome. Modern Trade has sat in a 18–25% band for 19 months while carrying a 20-point margin premium. Five points of shift is worth about 10 mn of gross profit a year on the same volume. Put a mix percentage in the plan and review it monthly.
  2. Push the 200 g pack. It realises 29% more per kilo than the 500 g at a lighter discount, and it is the format Modern Trade buys. This is the mechanism behind lever 1, so give it a volume target of its own.
  3. Interrogate the 24.1% WSF invoice discount. It is three and a half times what Modern Trade takes and it is given before any trade agreement. Ask whether it is buying anything — volume has been flat at 46–63 mn a month for 19 months, which suggests the discount is defending a position rather than growing one.
  4. Protect 7-Eleven FBV. At 66% it is the highest-margin outlet in the category and only 4.2 mn a month. It is also the customer that dilutes Ready, so the negotiation should be looked at across both categories, not one at a time.
  5. Explain the August -7%. Realisation is unchanged, so this is tonnes. FBV has only been below 76 mn twice in 19 months — Feb'25 and Feb'26, both Februaries — so it is worth confirming whether this is order timing or something real before the month closes.

Basis and caveats

Two volume bases — do not mix them. The master file states FBV volume on a dry basis (1,186 tonnes in Jul’26), while the billing extract records actual kilos of fresh product (2,510,840 kg in 24 days). The ratio is roughly 2.9×, so realisation figures are shown on their own basis and never compared across the two: THB ‘000 per dry tonne for the monthly series, THB per billed kilo for the August detail and every SKU, pack and channel table. Both are internally consistent; neither converts to the other in this analysis.

The mix-effect estimate holds July channel gross margins constant and applies August month-to-date channel shares, so it isolates mix from rate. It is an estimate, not an actual: August channel margins are not closed, and FBV trade spend is small enough (0.5% of NIV in July) that NIV and net sales are treated as near-identical for this calculation.

Margin figures are gross margin = gross profit ÷ net sales from the master file, July 2026 actual. Invoice discount is a different and additive measure: the ZD-series discount on the billing document itself, before any trade agreement.
